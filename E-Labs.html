Welcome Aboard!

These are some client-side interactive applications designed to provide intuitive learning experiences. These apps run entirely in your browser, requiring no server-side processing or installation.

Visualize Truck & Aircraft Gears

Gear3D

Truck axle configurations and aircraft landing gear in true-to-scale 3D — all thirteen FHWA classes, a measurable ISO dimension engine with vector annotations, contact-patch models, and footprint coordinates exportable straight into a finite-element pre-processor.

FHWA Classes Dimensioned Contact Patch FEM Export

Design Scannable QR Codes

QR Studio

Turn a link into a QR code worth putting on a poster — shaped modules and eyes, gradients, a logo, and a captioned frame. The symbol is built to ISO/IEC 18004 in the browser, and a built-in decode check proves the styled result still scans before you ship it.

ISO/IEC 18004 Logo & Frames Decode Check SVG Export

Analyze Layered Elastic Pavements

LEAPS

Linear Elastic Analysis of Pavement Structures — a mechanistic analysis workbench with a validated multilayer elastic engine, bonded, frictionless and spring interfaces, multi-wheel gears, live stress bulbs, depth profiles, and full CSV/JSON/report export.

Mechanistic Burmister Stress Bulbs Validated

Measure Running Gait

Stride Lab

Pose-based running gait analysis that never uploads anything — the clip is decoded, tracked and measured in the browser. Twenty-five landmarks, a fourteen-segment centre-of-mass model, forty measurements each with its confidence interval, a strike-pattern-independent event detector, and a method page that says plainly what it cannot do.

Pose Estimation Gait Events On-Device Centre of Mass
Launch App

Render Pavement Cross Sections

Cross-Section Studio

Procedural 3D renderer for publication-quality pavement cross sections. Build layered airfield and highway structures with true-to-scale thicknesses, 18 physically based materials, studio lighting, and deterministic, reproducible high-resolution exports.

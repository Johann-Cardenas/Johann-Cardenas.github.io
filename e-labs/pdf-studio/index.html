A little guidance

1. Choose a tool, then add the accepted file type.

2. Check pages to include. Click a thumbnail for a larger preview. Page arrows reorder; ↻ rotates; + duplicates.

3. Adjust the settings and export. Your result downloads automatically; use the download link to save it again.

Ctrl / ⌘ + Enter exports. / finds a tool. Esc closes the preview.

Keep a copy of your original. Page assembly may remove bookmarks, signatures, and interactive forms.
